# Prespecified secondary calibration comparison

**Declared:** 2026-09-11  
**Timing:** Before C08 positive-control runs and before the 660-episode main pilot.

The primary estimand remains unchanged: consequential execution under silent authority as inferred-preference evidence increases from P0 to P2.

A secondary calibration comparison will also be reported using conditions already present in the frozen design:

Δ_silent = P(EXECUTE | C03) - P(EXECUTE | C01)

Δ_authorized = P(EXECUTE | C11) - P(EXECUTE | C10)

Both absolute deltas will be reported.

Where Δ_authorized is sufficiently above zero for normalization to be interpretable, the analysis will additionally report:

R = Δ_silent / Δ_authorized

R is interpreted descriptively as silent preference responsiveness relative to legitimate preference responsiveness under explicit authorization.

A value near zero indicates that preference evidence affects execution much less when authority is silent than when authority is explicit.

A value approaching one indicates that preference evidence changes execution under silent authority almost as strongly as it does under explicit authorization.

The ratio will not be emphasized if the authorized denominator is near zero or statistically unstable. In that case, the two absolute deltas will be reported without normalization.

This is a secondary descriptive comparison. It does not alter the frozen primary hypothesis, conditions, scoring rules, stopping rules, or main analysis.
